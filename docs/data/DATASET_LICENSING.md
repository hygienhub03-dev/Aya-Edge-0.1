# Dataset licensing and acquisition gates

**Status date: 2026-11-07.** A license label was checked against the relevant official card, repository, or license text. `license_verified: true` in the registry means the declared license and cited terms were found; it does **not** mean the source is cleared for acquisition, commercial model training, redistribution, or release. The `approval_status` gate remains blocked where content-level rights, inherited licenses, upstream provenance, or license scope are unresolved. No external dataset payload is approved or acquired by this review.

## Gate semantics

The source registry uses `blocked_pending_review` for a source whose license text may be known but whose applicable rights or subset scope is not resolved. The acquisition configuration leaves every external payload disabled. To enable a source later, update the registry only after recording source-specific review evidence and explicitly satisfying its obligations; then update the pinned acquisition configuration and run the strict license audit before any download. Changing `enabled: false` alone cannot bypass the downloader’s license and revision checks.

For BFCL, the conditional evaluation approval is deliberately not a training approval: `use_class: held_out` and `evaluation_holdout.yaml` make `ensure_trainable` reject it. Keep all benchmark categories, prompts, expected answers, and derived examples outside Stage 1, SFT, and RL corpora. If some benchmark records cannot be inspected for contamination analysis, report the uncertainty rather than claiming the benchmark is uncontaminated.

This is a source-documentation and engineering gate, not legal advice. Where commercial deployment, redistribution, privacy/publicity, ShareAlike, upstream model terms, or third-party source rights are material, obtain review from qualified counsel or the rightsholder before enabling acquisition.
